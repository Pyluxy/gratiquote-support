Gratiquote Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Overview

Gratiquote is designed to provide daily inspirational quotes and a simple way to record and reflect on gratitude.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the app simple and private.

Information We Collect

Gratiquote does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

Your gratitude entries are stored locally on your device. Gratiquote does not transmit your gratitude entries to our servers.

Gratitude Entries

The gratitude entries you create are stored on your device so that you can view and edit your gratitude history.

Because this information is stored locally, removing the app or clearing its data may permanently remove your saved entries.

Notifications

Gratiquote may request permission to send notifications for daily quotes and gratitude reminders.

You can manage or disable notification permissions at any time through your device settings.

Third-Party Services

Gratiquote does not sell your personal information. The app may rely on operating-system and app-store services provided by Apple or Google for distribution and device functionality.

Children's Privacy

Gratiquote does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Gratiquote evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
