Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a gratitude entry?

Open the Gratitude tab, enter three things you're grateful for, and tap Save Gratitude.

Can I edit a previous gratitude entry?

Yes. Open the History tab, select the entry you want to change, make your corrections, and tap Save Changes.

Where can I see my previous entries?

Your saved gratitude entries are available in the History tab.

Does Gratiquote send reminders?

Gratiquote can send daily notifications to help you stay consistent with your daily quote and gratitude practice. Notification permissions can be managed in your device settings.
